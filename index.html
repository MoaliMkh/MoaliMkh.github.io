Recent and ongoing research

Chain-of-thought monitoring

Feb. 2026–present

Hidden reasoning and chain-of-thought monitoring

At LaCoCo Lab, I investigate when hidden computations leave detectable traces in a model's written reasoning. I led the experimental design and implementation, and analyze the findings in relation to theoretical predictions.

LaCoCo Lab · Saarland University · Supervised by Prof. Dr. Michael Hahn

Multimodal reasoning

Mar. 2026–present

Graph-based multimodal reasoning

At UKP Lab, I'm working on graph-based approaches to improve reasoning in vision-language models. My work includes method development and the design and execution of experiments.

UKP Lab · Darmstadt University · Supervised by Prof. Yue Feng

Interpretability

Fall 2025–winter 2025/26

Murano: mechanistic interpretability of LLMs

As a research assistant at UKP Lab, I helped design and develop a modular framework for reproducible mechanistic interpretability research. I implemented activation probing, activation patching, and steering-vector components to support systematic analysis and controlled interventions.

UKP Lab · Darmstadt University · Supervised by Prof. Iryna Gurevych
