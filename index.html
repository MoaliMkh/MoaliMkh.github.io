Questions I'm working on.

Chain-of-thought monitoring · Feb. 2026–present

What can we learn from the reasoning a model shows us?

At LaCoCo Lab, I led the experimental design and implementation of a project investigating when hidden computations leave detectable traces in a model's written reasoning. I analyze those findings alongside theoretical predictions to understand the possibilities and limits of monitoring.

LaCoCo Lab · Saarland University
Supervised by Prof. Dr. Michael Hahn

First-author preprint · under review ↗

Multimodal reasoning

Mar. 2026–present

Helping vision-language models reason with graphs

At UKP Lab, I'm leading research on graph-based approaches to reasoning in vision-language models, from reviewing the literature and developing methods to designing and running experiments.

UKP Lab · Darmstadt University · Supervised by Prof. Yue Feng

Interpretability

Fall 2025–winter 2025/26

Murano: tools for looking inside language models

As a research assistant at UKP Lab, I helped design and develop a modular framework for reproducible mechanistic interpretability research. I implemented activation probing, activation patching, and steering-vector components to support systematic analysis and controlled interventions.

UKP Lab · Darmstadt University · Supervised by Prof. Iryna Gurevych
